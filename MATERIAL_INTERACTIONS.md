# MVP Material Interaction Matrix

`Inert` means intentionally no meaningful MVP interaction.

| MATERIAL | WATER | HEAT | COLD | OXYGEN/FIRE | VIBRATION | SCREEN | WATER FLOW | MAGNETIC FIELD | CONTAINER BEHAVIOR | PHASE/REACTION | BYPRODUCTS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Sand | binds Water; excess stays free | dries; silica/iron fractions melt | no change | inert | mobilized | fines pass by aperture | constituents density-sort | iron-bearing fraction captured | retained by solid walls | mixture separates; silica/iron melt | concentrate, tailings, residue |
| Heavy/Fine Concentrate | slurry | dries/refines | no change | inert | mobilized | re-screenable | heavy fraction settles | magnetic fraction captured | granular | exact enrichment | tailings, pure quanta only when exact |
| Silica fraction | slurry constituent | molten glass above threshold | solidifies | inert | transported with host | size-dependent | density-sorted | nonmagnetic | contained as grain/liquid | Molten Glass → Glass | residue remains separate |
| Clay/Fines | binds Water strongly | bound Water leaves | no change | inert | mobilized | passes fine mesh | slow settling | nonmagnetic | wet sediment contained | conserved constituent; dedicated ceramic loop post-MVP | Water/Steam on drying |
| Stone/Rock Debris | displaces Water | conducts heat | inert | inert | weak motion | coarse retention | heavy settling | nonmagnetic | structural/granular | none | none |
| Coal | wetted surface | ignites/heats | inert | combusts with oxidizer | granular motion | size-dependent | settles | inert | stored physically | combustion | Smoke, Ash, heat |
| Wood | moisture changes ignition | dries/pyrolyzes | inert | burns | physical motion | coarse | floats/moves | inert | physical fuel | Charcoal in low oxygen | Smoke, Ash, vapor |
| Leaves | moisture changes ignition | dries/pyrolyzes | inert | burns | light motion | fine/coarse geometry | floats/moves | inert | loose matter | deterministic reaction | Smoke, Ash, vapor |
| Charcoal | can be wet | high-energy fuel | inert | burns | granular | size-dependent | moves | inert | stored physically | combustion | Smoke, Ash, heat |
| Ash | slurry/fines | inert | inert | noncombustible | fine motion | passes fine mesh | suspended/settles | inert | granular residue | none | none |
| Smoke | displaced by Steam/gases | buoyant gas | contracts only via gas model | product, not fuel | inert | passes permeable openings | displaced | inert | requires gas-tight geometry | atmosphere diffusion | none |
| Water | free/bound in sediment | boils | freezes | suppresses/cools fire | flows around mesh | passes openings | carrier for density separation | inert | requires liquid-tight geometry | Ice ↔ Water ↔ Steam | none |
| Steam | condenses | gains enthalpy/pressure | condenses | displaces oxidizer | inert | passes gas openings | gas flow | inert | pressure requires sealed Pipes/vessel | Steam ↔ Water | leaks physically |
| Glass | wetted only | melts | solid | inert | solid object/grain | aperture-dependent | heavy solid | nonmagnetic | liquid-tight component use | Glass ↔ Molten Glass | none |
| Molten Glass | quenched/cooled | remains molten | solidifies | inert | fluid motion | blocked by solid mesh | flows | nonmagnetic | requires heat-safe vessel | → Glass | none |
| Iron | wetted/thermal exchange | melts | solid | MVP oxidation inert | solid/grain motion | aperture-dependent | dense | magnetic | conductive vessel/wall | Iron ↔ Molten Iron | none |
| Molten Iron | cooled | remains molten | solidifies | inert | fluid motion | blocked by solid geometry | flows | constituent remains iron | requires refractory/ceramic containment | → Iron | slag/residue separate |
| Gold | wetted | thermally stable in MVP | solid | inert | grain motion | aperture-dependent | very dense | nonmagnetic | physical valuable material | exact recovery quantum | none |
| Raw Food | Water/Steam heat transfer | cooks over time | inert | burns at excess heat | physical cell | coarse | moves | inert | vessel optional, not recipe-bound | → Cooked/Burnt Food | none |
| Cooked Food | Water/Steam heat transfer | burns at excess exposure | inert | combustible at threshold | physical cell | coarse | moves | inert | physical cell | → Burnt Food | none |
| Burnt Food | wetted/cools | inert residue | inert | non-useful residue | physical cell | coarse | moves | inert | physical cell | terminal MVP state | none |

Dead-end audit: Burnt Food and Stone/Rock Debris are intentional terminal/residue materials. Clay fines are useful in sediment/wet separation and moisture accounting, but dedicated plastic-clay construction is frozen post-MVP. No new content branch is invented to disguise these boundaries.
