# MVP Scope Freeze

## MVP_CORE

- Shared Factory/Character/Creative world and deterministic WorldGen V2
- Granular Sand, provenance, exact composition, Water/Steam/gases, heat and phase changes
- Free-after-Research construction, Conveyors, storage, Pipes, physical leaks and power
- Component-built screening, wet density separation, magnetic separation, furnace/kiln, vessels and boiler
- Trees, Wood, Leaves, Coal, Charcoal, Fire, Smoke, Ash and physical cooking
- Research Bank, one shared progression, milestones, onboarding and recovery UX
- Main Menu, New/Continue/Load, Pause, Save/Save As/Delete, autosave and backup recovery
- Blueprints, automation, overlays, discovery and mobility

## MVP_OPTIONAL

- Food/cookware sandbox beyond onboarding
- Overview/debug overlays in normal builds
- Extended example Blueprint library
- Synthetic Megafactory stress fixture

## DEV_ONLY

- Legacy Crude Furnace, Sieve, Magnetic Separator, Wash Sluice and Iron Pot prefabs
- Correctness/benchmark fixtures, dense worlds, debug painting and diagnostics

## POST_MVP

- Advanced chemistry and material families
- Dedicated plastic Wet Clay/Dry Clay construction loop beyond conserved clay fines in sediment
- Farming, hunger, health, enemies, combat and large biome/content packs
- Nuclear industry
- Multiplayer and Web
- Public release operations, storefront work and broad external playtesting
- Advanced audio content beyond the bounded procedural playtest mix
